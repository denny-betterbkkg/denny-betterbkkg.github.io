Privacy Policy

Better Bookkeeping Workbench · Effective October 8, 2026

This policy explains how Better Bookkeeping LLC ("we", "us") handles data accessed through Better Bookkeeping Workbench (the "Application"), our internal tool that connects to QuickBooks Online.

1. Data we access

When a QuickBooks Online company is connected, the Application can read accounting data from that company, including the chart of accounts, vendors, customers, transactions (such as expenses, deposits, bills, invoices, and journal entries), and financial reports. The Application's access is read-only; it does not create, change, or delete records in QuickBooks Online.

2. How we use it

We use this data only to perform bookkeeping services for the business that owns the QuickBooks Online company — for example, suggesting how to categorize transactions and helping reconcile accounts. All suggestions are reviewed by Better Bookkeeping LLC personnel.

3. Where data is stored

Data retrieved by the Application is stored on computers controlled by Better Bookkeeping LLC. QuickBooks connection credentials are stored locally on those computers and are not shared.

4. Third-party processing

We do not sell data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. To assist with bookkeeping analysis, data may be processed by an AI assistant provided by Anthropic (Claude), acting as a service provider under its commercial terms. Data is sent to it only as needed to perform the bookkeeping work described above.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed to provide bookkeeping services or as required by law. A business may disconnect its QuickBooks Online company at any time and may ask us to delete data we hold about it by contacting us below.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect data, including restricting access to authorized personnel and keeping credentials out of shared or public locations.

7. Changes

We may update this policy by posting a revised version on this page.
